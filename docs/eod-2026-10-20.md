## 8. Commercial relevance

**Before this window:** a registered vFarm customer calling or messaging CST was silently downgraded to a guest and received a generic "a specialist will contact you shortly" — the support channel was, in effect, non-functional for anyone whose identity was not already resident in the adapter's memory. With the free tier rebooting ≈37× a day, that was the normal state, not an edge case.

**After the deploy:** the caller resolves to the **real, upstream-verified** vFarm identity (org admin, `owner`), the authorised farm scope comes from **vFarm's own grant list**, and farm-scoped questions are answered with farm-specific content. The grant already executed gives the account `operator` on the org's operational farm — the one with 26 devices — while the other farm, which belongs to a different account, is untouched.

**What is still not true:** no live voice call and no live SMS has been completed, so "a real customer reaches their farm data" is demonstrated **locally** (the end-to-end regression proves an authorised speaker reaches the upstream vFarm path with the authorised farm id, and a zero-grant speaker receives a safe reply with no upstream call) but **not yet in production**.
